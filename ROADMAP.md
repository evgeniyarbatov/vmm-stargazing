# Roadmap

Two jobs: make people curious about the night sky, and let them attach a memory to a constellation. The end of that path is thinking bigger — reaching for the stars. VMM is the first place that happens, not a generic ultra tool.

The site is a field notebook. It is not a planetarium.

## Now

Pre-computed notes for VMM 100 Miles 2026 as a public page: night windows, course and spots, sky discs at dusk / midnight / dawn, planets and constellations, chart and raw-data downloads.

## Next

Name spots after what is overhead, not sample order.

One sentence per disc: the thing you would notice if you stopped.

Constellation cards — a myth hook, and how to find it from that ridge.

Night 1 vs Night 2 as the same sky turning while you move.

## Later

Pace variants: if you are hours ahead, this star is here instead — effort meeting the sky.

Shareable night cards: one disc, one sentence, for after the race.

A place to write what you actually saw. Memory, not accuracy.

## Not this

A live planetarium. A heavy star catalogue. Weather APIs. A multi-race platform until VMM has a story worth retelling.
