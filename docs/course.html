While the course is astronomically dark, it is sampled every 2 km. Each point is scored for an open DSM horizon, higher ground, a dim or set moon, and whether the Milky Way centre sits above the local horizon. Aid stations are not used. The listed places are the highest-scoring points, spaced along the course so closer samples are the same stretch, not extra stops.
