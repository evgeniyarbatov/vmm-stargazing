While the course is astronomically dark, it is sampled every 2 km. Each point is scored for an open DSM horizon, higher ground, a dim or set moon, and whether the Milky Way centre sits above the ridge. Aid stations are not used. The listed places are the highest-scoring points at least 5 km apart (at most four per night). Closer samples are the same ridge, not extra stops. At realistic pace the second night is only the last kilometres of the course, so there are fewer places to stand — not a hidden second list.
